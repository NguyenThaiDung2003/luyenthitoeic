Questions 153-155 refer to the following internal memorandum and official corporate announcement.
INTERNAL MEMORANDUM
TRANS-PACIFIC LOGISTICS INC.
TO: All Department Heads
DATE: October 24, 2024
FROM: Eleanor Vance, VP of Supply Operations
SUBJECT: Launch of EcoPack Da Nang Hub

As part of our commitment to carbon-neutral regional distribution by 2026, I am delighted to announce the grand opening of our new EcoPack Distribution Center situated in the Hoa Khanh High-Tech Zone, Da Nang. This 45,000-square-meter facility marks the culmination of an eighteen-month modernization endeavor.

Unlike our legacy sites in Ho Chi Minh City and Hai Phong, the Da Nang center leverages state-of-the-art sorting automation that reduces packaging transit delays by approximately 35%. More importantly, the hub aims to reduce packaging carbon emissions by 40% across all central coastal routes within its first operational fiscal year.

To support this milestone, human resources has initiated a fast-track talent program. We plan to recruit 150 local technicians by next month to supervise automated robotics maintenance and inbound freight quality verification.

PRESS RELEASE: SUSTAINABLE EXPANSION

DA NANG, Nov. 2 — Trans-Pacific Logistics today cut the ribbon on its cutting-edge green hub. The facility features rooftop photovoltaic solar panels that supply up to 60% of the daily operational electricity during peak sunlight hours.

"Our customers have consistently requested eco-friendly packaging and reduced fulfillment footprints," remarked Eleanor Vance during the inaugural address. "The central corridor will now receive expedited two-day delivery without escalating our ecological footprint."
